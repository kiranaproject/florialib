## No vendored third-party source

`florialib` no longer contains any vendored third-party source code. The
path-clipping engine (`Floria.Path.Engine.Native`, `.Boolean`, `.Offset`) is
first-party MPL-2.0 code.

Earlier revisions vendored a Pascal port of Angus Johnson's **Clipper2**
(Boost Software License 1.0) as `floria.path.clipper.*`. That port has been
removed and no Clipper2 source, headers, or derivative code remains in the
tree.

The behavioural expectations that were originally validated against that port
are preserved as recorded numeric data in
`src/test/pascal/floria.path.golden.data.pas`. Those are geometric results
(areas and bounds) generated by running test inputs through the reference
implementation. They are not source code, not derived source, and not a
substantial portion of the original work; they are measurements of the output
of a program, comparable to a recorded test transcript.

## XCB bindings

The `Floria.XCB.*` and `Floria.X11.KeySym` units are generated bindings for the
X Window System's XCB libraries. XCB is developed by the X.Org Foundation and
is distributed under the MIT License. The generated Pascal declarations are
derived from the public XCB protocol headers.

Generated bindings do not change the licensing of the underlying libraries.
Applications linking `florialib` must also satisfy the licenses of the system
libraries they use.
